# 408-FLOW-2.2 — Universal Form-First Restoration

## Outcome

Every primary 408FARMERS property acquisition route begins with its lead form. A visitor is no longer required to answer marketing-engagement or context questions before reaching contact capture.

## Route behavior

- `/home/` presents its existing two-step contact/property form immediately. The prior three-question engagement and personalized payoff remain dormant for a later post-lead sprint.
- `/auto-bundle/`, `/healthcare/`, `/teachers/`, `/tech/`, and `/engineers/` render their complete forms directly instead of transforming the context field into a first-step gate.
- `/buyer/` already begins inside its property/contact form and remains unchanged.
- Contact, Neighbor, Life, operations, and non-acquisition surfaces remain unchanged. Life is an application-initialization experience, not part of the property lead-form restoration.

## Preserved boundaries

- Required identity, contact, property, route-context, and consent fields remain present.
- Formspree submission, attribution, confirmation, branching, zero-repeat prospect transfer, and CoverageFit launch behavior remain intact.
- CoverageFit v3.20.61 is synchronized without assessment, Question-Two runtime, recommendation, or Protection Score changes.
- No engagement field affects Protection Score.

## Next boundary

Post-form engagement questions and an optional invitation to continue into CoverageFit are intentionally outside this sprint.
