# 408-HOME-2.8 — Continuity, Branching & Recovery

HOME-2.8 makes the general Home journey resilient to refresh, back navigation, interrupted handoff, and incorrect renter entry without creating another assessment or lead form.

## Delivered

- A six-hour, same-tab checkpoint containing only the three bounded engagement answers, progress, and bounded flyer context.
- A visible Continue or Start Over choice. Pre-consent identity, contact, property, consent, and free-form data are never part of this checkpoint.
- One canonical branch resolver for owner-occupied, landlord, buyer, and renter contexts.
- A post-lead recovery panel that can relaunch the saved CoverageFit or renters destination without replaying the first lead submission.
- CoverageFit v3.20.59 trusted-profile recovery and recent-renter misroute protection.
- Preservation of CoverageFit’s existing seven-day assessment draft and all HOME-2.1 through HOME-2.7 behavior.

The sprint changes navigation resilience only. It does not change assessment questions, recommendations, Protection Score calculations, or the two-lead architecture.
