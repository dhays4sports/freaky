# 408-CRO-1.6.2.1 — Intent Payoff and Promise Alignment

## Outcome

Refined the complete 408FARMERS-to-CoverageFit journey so every handoff emphasizes the customer outcome—coverage clarity, meaningful priorities, and possible savings—without changing the truthful licensed-review boundary.

## Customer-facing changes

- Strengthened the homepage and Home route around what still fits, what may deserve a closer look, and what may be worth comparing.
- Reframed the buyer route around preventing last-minute closing questions and renamed the submission action `Continue My New-Home Review`.
- Balanced all four professional routes around both Protection Snapshot value and possible professional discounts.
- Shortened the progressive handoff so it explains the payoff before mechanics.
- Corrected the auto-bundle mismatch: homeowners continue into CoverageFit Home; renters keep the submitted lead and continue directly to Dylan through a renters-specific contact choice.

## Preserved contracts

- Initial 408FARMERS lead submission remains the first lead point.
- Completed CoverageFit Snapshot remains the second lead point for CoverageFit journeys.
- Slow or unavailable Formspree delivery does not block CoverageFit.
- Zero-repeat contact and consent reuse remain intact.
- CoverageFit assessment questions, Protection Score, recommendation rules, and licensed verification boundary are unchanged.
- No eligibility, discount, savings, rate, carrier-acceptance, or response-time guarantee was added.
